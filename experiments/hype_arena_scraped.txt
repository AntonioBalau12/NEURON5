Welcome to Hooly. 

Hooly is a fictional company built for this challenge. Its name nods to Hooli from Silicon Valley , while its products borrow real Google product names and place them in an entirely fictional universe. 

That universe spans a year of documents, web pages, social posts, presentations, and press releases. Prices change. People join and leave. Events happen. Dates matter. Some sources even contradict the truth. 

Your job is to figure out what the evidence actually supports.

The source material is your entire world. Don't rely on the live web or facts a pretrained model happens to know. A claim is only as reliable as the evidence in Hooly's universe. 

Two jobs. One system. 

Your system has two responsibilities, and both matter. 

Classify the rows 

Take every unlabelled row and assign one of three labels: 
True: the statement is true on its date. 

False: the statement contradicts the truth on its date. 

Unsure: the available evidence isn't enough to decide. 
Every true or false decision needs a confidence score between 0.5 and 1.0. 

Generate new claims 

Write exactly 1,000 new, structured claims about Hooly, connecting information across the universe. No claim may copy a row of the input word for word. Every generated claim must be true and original. 

How you build the system is up to you. How well it handles the evidence is what counts.

Confidence is part of the answer. 

Your system must communicate how confident it is. 

Every true or false label and every generated claim needs a confidence from 0.5 to 1.0. 
1.0 Certain 
Your system has strong evidence for its answer. 
0.75 Fairly sure 
The evidence supports the answer, but some uncertainty remains. 
0.5 A guess 
Your system is making its least-confident possible call. 

An unsure label has no confidence score. 

Five ways to make a claim. 

Generated claims must use one of five supported types. Each type represents a different way of expressing information from the source material. 
fact A direct statement about a subject. 
Hooly AI Pro costs €59 per month. 
compare A comparison between two subjects or values. 
Hooly Workspace costs more than Hooly AI Pro. 
change A statement describing how something changed over time. 
Hooly AI Pro went from €49 per month to €59 per month. 
at_event A fact tied to a specific event. 
Hooly Gemini spoke 12 languages at Hooly I/O 2026. 
relation A fact about whoever a link names, such as the CTO of Hooly. 
The CTO of Hooly joined on 1 July 2026. 

Every claim must be true. Every claim must be new. Submit exactly 1,000. Only the first 1,000 will be judged.

The build process has network access. The actual run does not.

What your system receives 

Approximately 20,000 rows. The practice rows retain their known labels; the remaining rows are unlabelled. 

What your system must return 

One CSV table containing a label for every unlabelled row and exactly 1,000 newly generated claims. 

What gets evaluated 

The table written to disk when the run ends. Make sure your output is complete, valid, and saved in the expected format.
